## Verdict

The saved selection is **close, but it is not the best version of this squad to submit**.

Three changes are justified before the deadline:

1. **Remove Enis Destan.** This is not a model opinion: official FPL marks him unavailable and says he has joined Konyaspor permanently. [Konyaspor announced a three-year contract](https://www.konyaspor.org.tr/haber/hos-geldin-enis-destan42). He is a guaranteed dead squad slot.
2. **Start Dara O'Shea; bench Justin Kluivert.** The live project model has O'Shea at 4.0 points and Kluivert at 3.7. The small numerical edge is supported by role and fixture: O'Shea is Ipswich captain at home to Sunderland, while Kluivert is away to Manchester City and has selection competition. This is a low-margin call, but the evidence all points the same way.
3. **Keep Haaland captain and change vice-captain from Raya to Saka.** The raw model narrowly favours Saka, 7.3 to 6.8, but that edge assumes only a 75% Saka start rate. Arsenal say Saka is ready to be involved, not that he will start; he played only 30 minutes in the Community Shield, and the current Fantasy Football Scout predicted XI omits him. Haaland started City's last competitive match, has the stronger minutes case and 69.4% ownership. The safer deadline-day decision is therefore Haaland (C), Saka (V).

**Raya**

Obi is the project's highest-rated £4.5m replacement, but this is a **legal-squad repair, not a claim that he is a secure starter**. His evidence base is thin and he was absent from United's final friendly squad. Keep him last on the bench.

## What changed the decision

### Captaincy: model upside versus start-risk

The latest local refresh (generated 11:48 UTC today) projects:

|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Saka | 7.3 | 75% | Arsenal say he is “ready to be involved”; only 30 Shield minutes after a late World Cup return | Vice-captain; high-upside but managed-minutes risk |
| Haaland | 6.8 | 84% | Started the Community Shield and played 53 minutes | Captain; stronger probability-weighted floor |

Arteta's exact availability answer was that Saka is ready to be involved ([official Arsenal press conference](https://www.arsenal.com/news/every-word-from-mikel-artetas-pre-coventry-presser-agLtL1y0uKj3)). In the [official Community Shield report](https://live.mancity.com/news/mens/arsenal-manchester-city-community-shield-match-report-63922483), Saka entered on 60 minutes, while Haaland started. The current [Fantasy Football Scout team-news prediction](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/team-news/) omits Saka; that is a third-party predicted lineup, not confirmation.

The official FPL Scout explicitly recommends captaining Haaland as early-season risk management, noting his huge ownership ([GW1 golden rules](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4685204)). Saka remains a defensible aggressive captain: Arsenal host promoted Coventry and the model gives him about 0.5 more raw expected points. But once the new non-start/limited-minutes evidence is weighted, that small edge is too fragile to justify overruling Haaland for this particular deadline.

Changing the vice-captain from Raya is unambiguously correct. If Haaland unexpectedly misses out, Saka has materially more haul potential than a goalkeeper.

### Guéhi is the largest outfield lineup risk

Guéhi is available in the official FPL feed and there is no registration problem: he appeared in the Community Shield. However, he began that match on City's bench and entered after 54 minutes. The current third-party FFS predicted XI also omits him. That makes him the least secure defender in the proposed XI.

This does **not** create a compelling last-minute transfer on the project's numbers: Guéhi is still projected for 4.9 GW1 points and no single transfer improves the best XI over the six-week horizon. It does mean bench order matters: put Kluivert first. If Guéhi plays zero minutes, Kluivert can auto-sub; if Guéhi makes a late cameo, that downside cannot be eliminated without replacing him.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep the Haaland structure.** The project’s no-Haaland optimum gains only about 2.7 projected XI points across GW1–6 and wins only narrowly in its simulation, while sacrificing the 69.4%-owned captain. That is not enough evidence for a deadline-day rebuild.
- **Do not chase the model's multi-transfer path.** Its own live digest says no single transfer improves the squad and describes the multi-move gain as too small to act on. Point estimates this close are not robust enough to justify changing the core.
- **Do not replace Saka solely because one predicted XI omits him.** The first-party statement is positive, Arsenal have the best immediate fixture, and Saka can still return from reduced minutes. Downgrade him from captain, not necessarily from the squad.
- **A Guéhi replacement is defensible but not proven superior.** His Shield benching is a real warning; the model still values City's home fixture and six-week run enough to retain him. If a late first-party City leak or manager comment says he will not start, replacing him becomes the priority after Destan.

## Final uncertainty and deadline checklist

No public source can make a predicted starting XI “definite.” The strongest unresolved uncertainties are Saka's starting/minutes load and Guéhi's starting place. The recommendation above deliberately separates confirmed facts (deadline, Destan transfer, official availability, competitive lineups) from predicted lineups and model estimates.

Immediately before 18:30 BST:

1. Re-open official FPL and confirm Destan has been replaced and the squad saves legally.
2. Check for any new Arsenal or City first-party update; only change Saka/Guéhi if the evidence is explicit.
3. Confirm **Haaland (C), Saka (V)**.
4. Confirm O'Shea starts, Kluivert is first outfield substitute, Reed second, £4.5m forward third.

### Method note

The project’s live digest was used as a decision aid, not as truth. It was generated at 11:48 UTC on deadline day and reports an out-of-sample rank correlation of about 0.46; small gaps such as O'Shea over Kluivert or Saka over Haaland should therefore be treated as directional. Primary-source availability and actual match participation were given more weight than model precision. Fantasy Football Scout was used only as a clearly labelled predicted-lineup warning where first-party sources cannot confirm a starting XI.
