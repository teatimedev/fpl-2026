# GW4 decision repair, 12 September 2026

The review found concrete defects behind the user's concern about continued
holds. These changes repair input and decision handling; they do not establish
better predictive performance or justify forcing more transfers.

## Findings and changes

- `overlay.py` research from 6 August was still multiplying both attacking
  rates indefinitely: Kluivert ×1.18, Foden ×1.08, Szoboszlai ×0.92. Kluivert's
  prose said he was Bournemouth's penalty taker while the current official
  listing put him second. Production no longer applies these unvalidated
  attacking adjustments. Current official set-piece orders replace stale
  player notes. The archived minutes prior still feeds the separately updated
  minutes model.
- The weekly planner compared only one unrestricted path against one hold
  path, then could overturn a separately recommended single on that basis.
  The September 12 published run even exact-scored the unrestricted result
  below its feasible hold alternative. The linear bench proxy can select a
  different path from the nonlinear score. We now explicitly compare a bounded
  shortlist of first actions with re-optimised continuations. Hold is included
  in selection, unrounded scores drive the threshold, and the buffer is applied
  to every action independently. A three-transfer package below its buffer
  cannot suppress a qualifying single. No global optimality is claimed.
- This-week simulation omitted hit costs. They now shift every simulated draw,
  so both the mean and win probability include hits. Copy identifies the
  one-week horizon and distinguishes it from the window comparison.
- The captain-change warning doubled the difference between the two players'
  projections. Both normal scores already count in the XI; changing captain
  changes only the extra copy. The warning now shows that single-copy margin
  and identifies that it excludes vice-captain fallback. The squad evaluator
  already handled the captain contribution separately.
- Public GW3 picks still contained Thiago after the user reported completing
  Thiago → João Pedro. A deadline-scoped user report now updates the planning
  squad, inferred bank and remaining transfers. It cannot overwrite another
  account or a changed public baseline, and never represents an authenticated
  lineup. For this report the estimated bank is £0.2m and two FT remain. Sale
  £7.9m and purchase £7.7m use official prices checked during the review.

## Validation boundaries

Regression checks cover stale multiplier isolation, current role notes,
approximate-path dominance reversals, a qualifying single versus an excessive
package, rounding near the threshold, hit effects in simulated outcomes,
user-report expiry, account/baseline conflicts and public refresh persistence.
The final test/build and browser verification results belong in the delivery
report; this document does not infer live deployment from local checks.

No new cameo probabilities, form weights, team-strength scaling or lower
transfer threshold were fitted. The baseline 20% conditional cameo probability
and historical rate shrinkage still need independent evaluation. Better
recommendation outcomes must be measured on future frozen forecasts or valid
historical deadline replays, not inferred from these regression tests.

Foden → Szoboszlai were not submitted by the assistant.
