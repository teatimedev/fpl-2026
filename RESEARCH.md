Compiled 6 August 2026. **Gameweek 1 deadline: Friday 21 August, 18:30 BST.**
All prices are pulled live from the official FPL API and are **locked until that
deadline** — nothing you see here will move before you pick.

Rules confirmed from the API's own `game_settings`: **£100.0m budget, 15 players
(2 GKP / 5 DEF / 5 MID / 3 FWD), maximum 3 per club**, 11 must start.

## 1. What has actually changed this season

### The league
Burnley, West Ham and Wolves went down. **Coventry City, Hull City and Ipswich
Town** came up. Arsenal are the reigning champions.

### Rule changes
1. **Live bonus points** — projected bonus appears after 20 minutes of a match.
   Mini-league standings and overall rank also update live.
2. **BPS rebalanced** to reduce overlap with defensive contributions. Being
   tackled no longer costs BPS (helps dribblers), and clearances/blocks/
   interceptions now earn 1 BPS per *three* actions instead of per two. Net
   effect: **better bonus prospects for goalkeepers, full-backs and attackers**,
   worse for pure DefCon specialists.
3. **Later lockdown** — scores finalise at 9am the following day.
4. **No extra AFCON transfers** — AFCON falls in June/July 2027, so there is no
   December transfer bonus this season.
5. **Percentage rank display** added.

**Defensive Contributions (DefCon) are unchanged**: 10 qualifying actions for
defenders (clearances, blocks, interceptions, tackles), 12 for midfielders and
forwards (those four plus recoveries), capped at +2 per match. This remains one
of the largest scoring sources in the game and it drives a lot of the model
below.

## 3. Pre-season form

Friendlies are noisy, but minutes and roles are signal. What stood out:

- **Justin Kluivert (BOU, £6.0m)** — hat-trick including **two penalties** in a
  10-1 win over Genoa. With Kroupi injured he looks like Bournemouth's spot-kick
  taker. Alex Scott (£6.0m) scored twice in the same game.
- **Bryan Mbeumo (MUN, £8.0m)** — two goals against Atlético including a
  penalty, playing centrally. He is second on United's pen order behind Bruno.
- **Igor Jesus (NFO, £6.0m)** — four goals in four games.
- **Antoine Semenyo (MCI, £8.5m)** — assisted against Inter, but Maresca is
  using him **wide** rather than centrally. His own words: "Enzo is just about
  being out wide." That is a genuine downgrade risk on last season's 202 points.
- **Phil Foden (MCI, £7.0m)** — captained City against Inter and has been named
  one of Maresca's "three central pillars".
- **Dominik Szoboszlai (LIV, £7.0m)** — 1 goal 2 assists, but Iraola is using
  him **deeper**. Still on corners and free-kicks.
- **Dominic Calvert-Lewin (LEE, £6.0m)** — brace in pre-season after 14 Premier
  League goals last season. Only went up £0.5m.
- **Chelsea** — João Pedro 4 goals, Gittens 3 goals and 2 assists, Estêvão
  scored against Spurs.
- **Villa** — Buendía 2 goals and 3 assists; Kamara scored on his return from
  seven months out. But **McGinn limped off with a knee injury** and Garnacho
  came off after a blow to the mouth.
- **Merlin Röhl (EVE, £5.0m)** — scored from the right, carrying end-of-season
  form into pre-season. Cheap access to Everton's good opening run.
- **Fulham's penalties are unresolved** — Muniz and Iwobi have both converted
  since Jiménez left.

## 8. Traps worth knowing about

- **Dúbravka (TOT, £4.0m) is not Spurs' goalkeeper.** His 3,150 minutes last
  season were at Burnley. De Zerbi has confirmed **Antonín Kinsky (£4.5m)** as
  his number one and given him a new long-term contract; Vicario is being sold.
  Dúbravka is a £4.0m non-playing bench enabler and nothing more — which is
  exactly why 24% of managers have him.
- **Elliot Anderson's DefCon output should fall, not rise.** He led the league
  with 515 qualifying defensive actions (13.9 per 90 against a threshold of 12)
  — but he did that at Nottingham Forest. At Man City, who will dominate
  possession, there is simply less defending to do. He is still a strong pick at
  £6.5m; just don't price in another 180-point season.
- **Semenyo's role has changed.** 202 points last season, now being played wide
  by Maresca. At £8.5m that is a lot of money for a role change.
- **The BPS rebalance quietly nerfs DefCon specialists** — clearances now earn
  1 BPS per three actions rather than per two, so the same defending yields
  fewer bonus points.
- **The window shuts on 1 September**, after GW1 and GW2.
